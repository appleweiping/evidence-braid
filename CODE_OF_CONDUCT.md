# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Accept responsibility, apologize to those affected by mistakes, and learn.
- Focus on what is best for the overall community.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, trolling, publication of
private information, and other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate are
not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers through the
repository's security contact. Maintainers will review reports promptly,
respect the reporter's privacy, and take corrective action proportionate to the
conduct. Maintainers who do not follow this Code of Conduct may be removed from
the project team.

This Code applies in project spaces and when an individual officially
represents the project in public spaces.
